How it was built

  • Data: PhysioNet/CinC Challenge 2019, 40,336 ICU patients and 1,552,210 hourly records from two hospital systems.
  • Label: Sepsis-3 onset, moved 6 hours earlier so the model learns to warn ahead of time.
  • Signals: 124 per hour, including vital-sign trends, latest labs, hours since each test, testing intensity, and SIRS, qSOFA and partial SOFA scores. Built only from past hours and checked for leakage.
  • Model: LightGBM tuned with Optuna. Trained on 70% of Hospital A; tested on unseen Hospital A patients and all of Hospital B.

Limitations

  • Two of the strongest signals describe the care process (time in ICU, when oxygen settings were last charted), and care habits differ between hospitals.
  • Most labs are rarely measured. SOFA is partial, with no consciousness score or urine output.
  • The alert line has to be re-checked at every new hospital before use.
  • Research demo only. Not validated for clinical care.
